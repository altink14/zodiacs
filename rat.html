Years of the Rat 鼠年

A zodiac year starts at Chinese New Year (late January to mid-February), not on January 1 — so people born in early January or February belong to the previous animal. Each year also carries one of the five elements from the ten heavenly stems, which is why the same animal returns as a different element every twelve years and the full cycle is sixty.

The Great Race 十二生肖的传说

1place in the race

The Jade Emperor held a great race to decide the order of the zodiac. The Rat, a poor swimmer, persuaded the kind Ox to carry it across the river — then leapt from the Ox's head at the far bank and finished first.

Traditional character 性格

In Chinese folk tradition, people born in a Rat year are said to share the animal's temperament. These are cultural attributions, not predictions.

Said to be

  • quick-witted
  • resourceful
  • adaptable
  • sociable
  • thrifty

Watch out for

  • opportunistic
  • restless
  • overly cautious with money
