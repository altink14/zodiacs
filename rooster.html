At a glance 地支 · 酉 yǒu

The Rooster is the tenth of the twelve signs and is paired with the earthly branch 酉 (yǒu). The branch fixes its two-hour slot, its month, its compass point and its element.

Years of the Rooster 鸡年

A zodiac year starts at Chinese New Year (late January to mid-February), not on January 1 — so people born in early January or February belong to the previous animal. Each year also carries one of the five elements from the ten heavenly stems, which is why the same animal returns as a different element every twelve years and the full cycle is sixty.

The Great Race 十二生肖的传说

10place in the race

The Rooster spotted the raft that carried it, the Goat and the Monkey across the river. For finding the raft and crossing as a team, the Rooster was named tenth.

Traditional character 性格

In Chinese folk tradition, people born in a Rooster year are said to share the animal's temperament. These are cultural attributions, not predictions.

Said to be

  • observant
  • hardworking
  • confident
  • punctual
  • courageous

Watch out for

  • vain
  • critical
  • boastful
