Sep 23 – Oct 22 Cardinal Air

At a glance

Libra is the seventh sign of the zodiac, symbolised by the scales. It is a cardinal air sign — communicative, curious, social, and among the cardinal signs, the initiators.

In the sky

The sign shares its name with a constellation, but the two are not the same thing. Astrological signs are twelve equal 30° slices of the Sun's yearly path measured from the spring equinox; the constellations are irregular in size and, because of the slow wobble of Earth's axis, the Sun now passes through the constellation Libra roughly a month after the dates above.

The myth

♎The Scales

Libra is the only zodiac sign represented by an object rather than a living creature. The scales are those of Themis or Astraea, goddesses of justice, and the sign begins at the autumn equinox, when day and night are equal.
