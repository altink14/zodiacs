At a glance 地支 · 未 wèi

The Goat is the eighth of the twelve signs and is paired with the earthly branch 未 (wèi). The branch fixes its two-hour slot, its month, its compass point and its element.

Years of the Goat 羊年

A zodiac year starts at Chinese New Year (late January to mid-February), not on January 1 — so people born in early January or February belong to the previous animal. Each year also carries one of the five elements from the ten heavenly stems, which is why the same animal returns as a different element every twelve years and the full cycle is sixty.

The Great Race 十二生肖的传说

8place in the race

The Goat, Monkey and Rooster found a raft and worked together to pole it across the river. The Jade Emperor was so pleased by their teamwork that he named them eighth, ninth and tenth.

Traditional character 性格

In Chinese folk tradition, people born in a Goat year are said to share the animal's temperament. These are cultural attributions, not predictions.

Said to be

  • gentle
  • creative
  • calm
  • sympathetic
  • persevering

Watch out for

  • indecisive
  • pessimistic
  • dependent on others
