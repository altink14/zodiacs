At a glance

Aquarius is the eleventh sign of the zodiac, symbolised by the water-bearer. It is a fixed air sign — communicative, curious, social, and among the fixed signs, the sustainers.

In the sky

The sign shares its name with a constellation, but the two are not the same thing. Astrological signs are twelve equal 30° slices of the Sun's yearly path measured from the spring equinox; the constellations are irregular in size and, because of the slow wobble of Earth's axis, the Sun now passes through the constellation Aquarius roughly a month after the dates above.

The myth

♒The Water-Bearer

The water-bearer is Ganymede, a beautiful Trojan youth carried off by Zeus to serve as cup-bearer to the gods. Despite the name, Aquarius is an air sign — the water he pours is knowledge shared freely.
