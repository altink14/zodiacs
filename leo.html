At a glance

Leo is the fifth sign of the zodiac, symbolised by the lion. It is a fixed fire sign — energetic, spontaneous, passionate, and among the fixed signs, the sustainers.

In the sky

The sign shares its name with a constellation, but the two are not the same thing. Astrological signs are twelve equal 30° slices of the Sun's yearly path measured from the spring equinox; the constellations are irregular in size and, because of the slow wobble of Earth's axis, the Sun now passes through the constellation Leo roughly a month after the dates above.

The myth

♌The Lion

Leo is the Nemean Lion, a beast with an impenetrable hide that Heracles slew as the first of his twelve labours. Its brightest star, Regulus, means 'little king' and sits almost exactly on the ecliptic.
