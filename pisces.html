At a glance

Pisces is the twelfth sign of the zodiac, symbolised by the fish. It is a mutable water sign — emotional, intuitive, sensitive, and among the mutable signs, the adapters.

In the sky

The sign shares its name with a constellation, but the two are not the same thing. Astrological signs are twelve equal 30° slices of the Sun's yearly path measured from the spring equinox; the constellations are irregular in size and, because of the slow wobble of Earth's axis, the Sun now passes through the constellation Pisces roughly a month after the dates above.

The myth

♓The Fish

Pisces is two fish tied together by a cord. In Greek myth they carried Aphrodite and her son Eros to safety when the monster Typhon attacked, and were placed in the sky as thanks.
