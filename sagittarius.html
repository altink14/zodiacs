At a glance

Sagittarius is the ninth sign of the zodiac, symbolised by the archer. It is a mutable fire sign — energetic, spontaneous, passionate, and among the mutable signs, the adapters.

In the sky

The sign shares its name with a constellation, but the two are not the same thing. Astrological signs are twelve equal 30° slices of the Sun's yearly path measured from the spring equinox; the constellations are irregular in size and, because of the slow wobble of Earth's axis, the Sun now passes through the constellation Sagittarius roughly a month after the dates above.

The myth

♐The Archer

The archer is a centaur, usually identified as Crotus, inventor of archery, though he is sometimes confused with the wise centaur Chiron. The constellation lies toward the centre of the Milky Way galaxy.
