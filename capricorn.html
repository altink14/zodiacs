At a glance

Capricorn is the tenth sign of the zodiac, symbolised by the sea-goat. It is a cardinal earth sign — grounded, practical, steady, and among the cardinal signs, the initiators.

In the sky

The sign shares its name with a constellation, but the two are not the same thing. Astrological signs are twelve equal 30° slices of the Sun's yearly path measured from the spring equinox; the constellations are irregular in size and, because of the slow wobble of Earth's axis, the Sun now passes through the constellation Capricorn roughly a month after the dates above.

The myth

♑The Sea-Goat

Capricorn is a goat with a fish's tail. It is linked to the god Pan, who dove into the Nile to escape the monster Typhon and half-transformed into a fish, and to Amalthea, the goat that nursed the infant Zeus.
