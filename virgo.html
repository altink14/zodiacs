At a glance

Virgo is the sixth sign of the zodiac, symbolised by the maiden. It is a mutable earth sign — grounded, practical, steady, and among the mutable signs, the adapters.

In the sky

The sign shares its name with a constellation, but the two are not the same thing. Astrological signs are twelve equal 30° slices of the Sun's yearly path measured from the spring equinox; the constellations are irregular in size and, because of the slow wobble of Earth's axis, the Sun now passes through the constellation Virgo roughly a month after the dates above.

The myth

♍The Maiden

The maiden is usually identified with Demeter, goddess of the harvest, or her daughter Persephone, and sometimes with Dike, goddess of justice. Spica, its brightest star, represents an ear of wheat in her hand.
