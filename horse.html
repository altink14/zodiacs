At a glance 地支 · 午 wǔ

The Horse is the seventh of the twelve signs and is paired with the earthly branch 午 (wǔ). The branch fixes its two-hour slot, its month, its compass point and its element.

Years of the Horse 马年

A zodiac year starts at Chinese New Year (late January to mid-February), not on January 1 — so people born in early January or February belong to the previous animal. Each year also carries one of the five elements from the ten heavenly stems, which is why the same animal returns as a different element every twelve years and the full cycle is sixty.

7place in the race

Traditional character 性格

In Chinese folk tradition, people born in a Horse year are said to share the animal's temperament. These are cultural attributions, not predictions.

Said to be

  • lively
  • independent
  • warm-hearted
  • energetic
  • straightforward

Watch out for

  • impatient
  • restless
  • fickle
