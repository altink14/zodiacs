At a glance 地支 · 亥 hài

The Pig is the twelfth of the twelve signs and is paired with the earthly branch 亥 (hài). The branch fixes its two-hour slot, its month, its compass point and its element.

Years of the Pig 猪年

A zodiac year starts at Chinese New Year (late January to mid-February), not on January 1 — so people born in early January or February belong to the previous animal. Each year also carries one of the five elements from the ten heavenly stems, which is why the same animal returns as a different element every twelve years and the full cycle is sixty.

The Great Race 十二生肖的传说

12place in the race

Halfway through the race the Pig got hungry, stopped for a feast, and fell asleep. It woke, finished the crossing, and arrived twelfth — the last animal to earn a place in the zodiac.

Traditional character 性格

In Chinese folk tradition, people born in a Pig year are said to share the animal's temperament. These are cultural attributions, not predictions.

Said to be

  • generous
  • easygoing
  • sincere
  • diligent
  • compassionate

  • indulgent
