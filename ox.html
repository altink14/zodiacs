At a glance 地支 · 丑 chǒu

The Ox is the second of the twelve signs and is paired with the earthly branch 丑 (chǒu). The branch fixes its two-hour slot, its month, its compass point and its element.

Years of the Ox 牛年

A zodiac year starts at Chinese New Year (late January to mid-February), not on January 1 — so people born in early January or February belong to the previous animal. Each year also carries one of the five elements from the ten heavenly stems, which is why the same animal returns as a different element every twelve years and the full cycle is sixty.

The Great Race 十二生肖的传说

2place in the race

The Ox was the strongest swimmer and should have won the race. It generously let the Rat ride on its head, and was overtaken at the very last moment — earning an honest second place.

Traditional character 性格

In Chinese folk tradition, people born in a Ox year are said to share the animal's temperament. These are cultural attributions, not predictions.

Said to be

  • diligent
  • dependable
  • patient
  • honest
  • strong-willed

Watch out for

  • stubborn
  • slow to change
  • reserved
