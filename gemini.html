May 21 – Jun 20 Mutable Air

At a glance

Gemini is the third sign of the zodiac, symbolised by the twins. It is a mutable air sign — communicative, curious, social, and among the mutable signs, the adapters.

In the sky

The sign shares its name with a constellation, but the two are not the same thing. Astrological signs are twelve equal 30° slices of the Sun's yearly path measured from the spring equinox; the constellations are irregular in size and, because of the slow wobble of Earth's axis, the Sun now passes through the constellation Gemini roughly a month after the dates above.

The myth

♊The Twins

Gemini represents the twins Castor and Pollux. Pollux was immortal, Castor was not; when Castor died, Pollux begged Zeus to share his immortality, and the brothers were placed in the sky together.

Traditional character
