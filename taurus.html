At a glance

Taurus is the second sign of the zodiac, symbolised by the bull. It is a fixed earth sign — grounded, practical, steady, and among the fixed signs, the sustainers.

In the sky

The sign shares its name with a constellation, but the two are not the same thing. Astrological signs are twelve equal 30° slices of the Sun's yearly path measured from the spring equinox; the constellations are irregular in size and, because of the slow wobble of Earth's axis, the Sun now passes through the constellation Taurus roughly a month after the dates above.

The myth

♉The Bull

The bull is most often linked to Zeus, who took the form of a magnificent white bull to carry the princess Europa across the sea to Crete. The constellation also holds two famous star clusters, the Pleiades and the Hyades.

Traditional character
