At a glance

Scorpio is the eighth sign of the zodiac, symbolised by the scorpion. It is a fixed water sign — emotional, intuitive, sensitive, and among the fixed signs, the sustainers.

In the sky

The sign shares its name with a constellation, but the two are not the same thing. Astrological signs are twelve equal 30° slices of the Sun's yearly path measured from the spring equinox; the constellations are irregular in size and, because of the slow wobble of Earth's axis, the Sun now passes through the constellation Scorpio roughly a month after the dates above.

The myth

♏The Scorpion

The scorpion was sent by Gaia (or Artemis) to kill the boastful hunter Orion. Both were placed in the sky on opposite sides, so that Orion sets in the west as Scorpius rises in the east, forever fleeing.
