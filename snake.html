At a glance 地支 · 巳 sì

The Snake is the sixth of the twelve signs and is paired with the earthly branch 巳 (sì). The branch fixes its two-hour slot, its month, its compass point and its element.

Years of the Snake 蛇年

A zodiac year starts at Chinese New Year (late January to mid-February), not on January 1 — so people born in early January or February belong to the previous animal. Each year also carries one of the five elements from the ten heavenly stems, which is why the same animal returns as a different element every twelve years and the full cycle is sixty.

The Great Race 十二生肖的传说

6place in the race

The Snake hid coiled around the Horse's hoof for the whole crossing. At the bank it slithered out, startled the Horse, and slipped across the finish line in sixth place.

Traditional character 性格

In Chinese folk tradition, people born in a Snake year are said to share the animal's temperament. These are cultural attributions, not predictions.
