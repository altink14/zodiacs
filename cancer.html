At a glance

Cancer is the fourth sign of the zodiac, symbolised by the crab. It is a cardinal water sign — emotional, intuitive, sensitive, and among the cardinal signs, the initiators.

In the sky

The sign shares its name with a constellation, but the two are not the same thing. Astrological signs are twelve equal 30° slices of the Sun's yearly path measured from the spring equinox; the constellations are irregular in size and, because of the slow wobble of Earth's axis, the Sun now passes through the constellation Cancer roughly a month after the dates above.

The myth

♋The Crab

The crab was sent by Hera to distract Heracles during his battle with the Hydra. Heracles crushed it, and Hera placed the crab among the stars in gratitude for its loyalty.
