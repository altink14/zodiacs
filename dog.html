At a glance 地支 · 戌 xū

The Dog is the eleventh of the twelve signs and is paired with the earthly branch 戌 (xū). The branch fixes its two-hour slot, its month, its compass point and its element.

Years of the Dog 狗年

A zodiac year starts at Chinese New Year (late January to mid-February), not on January 1 — so people born in early January or February belong to the previous animal. Each year also carries one of the five elements from the ten heavenly stems, which is why the same animal returns as a different element every twelve years and the full cycle is sixty.

The Great Race 十二生肖的传说

11place in the race

The Dog was one of the best swimmers in the race, but the water was so clean and fresh that it stopped to play and bathe — and only remembered the race in time to finish eleventh.

Traditional character 性格

In Chinese folk tradition, people born in a Dog year are said to share the animal's temperament. These are cultural attributions, not predictions.

Said to be

  • loyal
  • honest
  • just
  • reliable
  • protective

Watch out for

  • anxious
  • stubborn
  • critical of others
