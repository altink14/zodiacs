At a glance 地支 · 卯 mǎo

The Rabbit is the fourth of the twelve signs and is paired with the earthly branch 卯 (mǎo). The branch fixes its two-hour slot, its month, its compass point and its element.

Years of the Rabbit 兔年

A zodiac year starts at Chinese New Year (late January to mid-February), not on January 1 — so people born in early January or February belong to the previous animal. Each year also carries one of the five elements from the ten heavenly stems, which is why the same animal returns as a different element every twelve years and the full cycle is sixty.

The Great Race 十二生肖的传说

4place in the race

The Rabbit hopped from stone to stone across the river. When the stones ran out it clung to a floating log, which the Dragon kindly blew to shore with a puff of breath — fourth place.

Traditional character 性格

In Chinese folk tradition, people born in a Rabbit year are said to share the animal's temperament. These are cultural attributions, not predictions.

Said to be

  • gentle
  • elegant
  • alert
  • considerate
  • artistic

Watch out for

  • timid
  • conflict-avoidant
  • overly cautious
